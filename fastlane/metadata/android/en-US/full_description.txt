Pele Shaon Luach is a Jewish calendar and clock that works fully offline.

JEWISH CALENDAR
• Jewish month grid with civil dates, festivals, fasts, Rosh Chodesh, Chanukah, Omer and the weekly Torah portion
• Diaspora or Israel schedule according to your city
• Any day: times of the day, candle lighting and Shabbat/Yom Tov end

MY REMINDERS
• Add, edit and delete your own reminders right in the calendar
• Repeat by civil date or by Jewish date — yahrzeits, Jewish birthdays, every Rosh Chodesh
• Import from Google Calendar (its ZIP/.ics export) and export to standard .ics and .vcs files

ZMANIM
• Times of the day by the method of the Alter Rebbe, Litvish or Sephardi custom
• Clock face with halachic hours, dawn, end of Shema, sunset and nightfall
• Hundreds of cities, or enter coordinates yourself

ALARMS THAT RESPECT SHABBAT
• Morning alarm at a set time or before the end of the Shema time
• Torah study alarm and your own alarms
• Silent from candle lighting until Shabbat and Yom Tov end; optional silence on Chol HaMoed
• Six-hour meat-to-dairy timer

ALSO
• Kiddush Levana reminder with a Moon compass and the direction to Jerusalem
• Home-screen widgets, night screen and screensaver
• Wear OS app

PRIVACY
No ads, no accounts, no analytics. The app has no internet permission; everything stays on your phone.

This is a beta version. Religious times are calculated, not certified — confirm local customs with your rabbi.
